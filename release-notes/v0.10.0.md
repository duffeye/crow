Faster GPS telemetry for Crow Camera on Hammerhead Karoo 3.

Download **crow-v0.10.0.apk** under Assets; a GitHub account is not required.

## Changes

- Restore up to **10 GPS updates per second**, replacing the 1 Hz battery-saving cap in 0.9.0 after reported telemetry-overlay lag.
- Request high-priority Bluetooth while GPS is actively forwarding. Return to low-power Bluetooth when GPS pauses or stops.
- Display measured usable-fix and completed GPS-packet rates in the app and notification: **GPS sent • fixes X Hz / BLE Y Hz**. The actual rate depends on Karoo hardware and camera firmware; 10 Hz is not guaranteed.
- Reject GPS fixes already more than one second old to avoid sending delayed positions.
- Retain automatic GPS pause/resume for unavailable or sleeping cameras, screen-off/unchanged-field optimizations, low-power filtered scanning and camera command priority.

GPS forwarding will use more power than 0.9.0 while active. It remains off by default. Crow forwards genuinely new measured fixes with their original timestamps; it does not duplicate or interpolate positions to manufacture a 10 Hz stream. Samples are dropped rather than queued behind camera commands.

## Test on your camera

Record a short outdoor clip including acceleration, braking and turns. Note the measured fix/BLE rates, then check overlay smoothness and alignment in DJI Mimo. If usable fixes stay near 1 Hz, the platform may not supply faster data. A much lower BLE rate indicates the link is limiting completed writes. A constant overlay time offset may need separate timestamp/Mimo investigation. Successful BLE writes do not prove the camera embedded GPS metadata.

Action 4 support is retained; Action 5 Pro and Action 6 remain experimental. GPS and end-to-end overlay timing need physical-device validation.

Sideload using USB debugging, for example `adb install -r crow-v0.10.0.apk`. This is a debug-signed test APK. If Android rejects an update due to a different signing key, uninstall the previous build, install this APK, and pair/configure Crow again. Keep your working APK as a fallback.

See the [README](https://github.com/duffeye/crow#faster-gps-overlays-0100) for setup and limitations. Crow is independent of DJI and Hammerhead.
